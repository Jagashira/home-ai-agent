Home Mail Agent

A personal AI-assisted email organization tool.

Home Mail Agent is a personal-use application designed to help its owner organize email across authorized Gmail accounts.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

Current capabilities

Designed for a clearer inbox

  • Access authorized Gmail accounts
  • Read email metadata and message content
  • Classify incoming email
  • Identify important, actionable, job-related, security, billing, service, and promotional messages
  • Add organizational Gmail labels
  • Generate a local daily email digest

Human review remains important

Home Mail Agent does not automatically send email, delete email, or permanently remove messages. Automated classifications and summaries may contain errors and should be reviewed by the user.
